# 3D-Printed Enclosure

![](images/Pasted%20image%2020260209153017.png)

This 3D-printed enclosure was successfully printed on a **Bambu Lab P1S** using a **0.4 mm nozzle** and **PLA filament**.

**Recommended print settings** (the ones used for the model shown):
- Nozzle: 0.4 mm
- Layer height: 0.2 mm
- Infill: 15–20% (Gyroid or Grid works well)
- Walls: 3–4 perimeters
- Supports: Not required (model is designed to print support-free)
- Material: Standard PLA (any brand; the photos used Bambu Lab Basic PLA)
- Print time: ~4–6 hours (depending on settings and exact model size)

The enclosure fits perfectly, has good dimensional accuracy, and the surface finish is smooth straight off the P1S bed with default PLA profile in Bambu Studio. Feel free to adjust infill or wall thickness if you need extra strength. No post-processing was required.

# Panel

The translucent black panel was precision laser-cut from high-quality smoked (translucent) black acrylic sheet.  

**Material**: Translucent black acrylic (allows light to pass through while maintaining a dark, sleek appearance—ideal for LED indicators or displays)  
**Dimensions**: 25.6 mm × 10 mm  
**Thickness**: 2 mm (standard for clean laser cutting and good light diffusion)  
**Cutting method**: Professional CO₂ laser cutting for perfectly smooth edges and high dimensional accuracy  

The panel fits flush into the 3D-printed enclosure, providing an elegant semi-transparent window that diffuses light evenly without revealing internal components. No additional finishing was required after cutting.
